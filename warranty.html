Quality Assurance & Warranty Terms

Home / Warranty

Thank you for choosing Crystal Salt Industry Co. We are firmly committed to providing world-class salt refining services, adhering to precise chemical parameters, and ensuring complete contractual satisfaction. Please review our service guarantee and quality assurance terms below.

1. Ensuring Quality of Product & Service

We work diligently to ensure all exported salt batches conform strictly to the technical specifications defined in the sales contract and proforma invoice. This covers NaCl purity percentage, moisture thresholds, granulation sizing, water-insoluble matter, and anti-caking treatment. If any verified discrepancy arises regarding quality during the warranty and inspection window, our commercial team will immediately take the necessary corrective steps to guarantee client satisfaction.

2. Warranty Period & Pre-Shipment Inspection

The quality guarantee covers the product from the moment of dispatch through arrival at the discharge port, provided standard maritime storage conditions are observed. The formal verification period is determined by the purchase agreement and maritime bill of lading date. Buyers are encouraged to appoint an independent inspection surveyor (e.g., SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) at the port of loading to confirm chemical and weight compliance before vessel departure.

3. Warranty Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:

  • Damage or moisture degradation resulting from improper maritime vessel hatch sealing or unauthorized handling by third-party stevedores.
  • Inappropriate or negligent warehouse storage at destination ports (e.g., unshielded exposure to open rainwater or excessive humidity without pallets).
  • Force majeure events beyond reasonable commercial control, including extreme maritime weather, port strikes, or geopolitical navigation disruptions.

4. Warranty & Quality Claim Procedures

In the rare event of a quality dispute, the customer must:

  • Notify our commercial desk in writing within 14 calendar days of cargo arrival at the destination port.
  • Submit relevant discharge survey reports and certified laboratory test certificates from an internationally accredited inspection agency.
  • Provide photographic evidence of packaging condition, container seal numbers, or vessel hold sampling methodology.

5. Binding Standards

These terms represent a binding commercial framework between Crystal Salt Industry Co. and our international clientele. By entering into supply agreements or receiving shipments, you confirm your acceptance of these standards.
