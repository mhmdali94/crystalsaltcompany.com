Our Corporate Heritage

Running a Successful Salt Manufacturing & Export Business Since 2000

Crystal Salt Industry Company is an accredited manufacturer, bulk exporter, and international supplier in Egypt, dedicated to producing an extensive range of high-quality crystalline salt products. We specialize in raw sea salt, triple-washed industrial salt, food-grade vacuum salt, pharmaceutical iodized salt, and high-purity water-sterilizing salt pellets.

We are recognized pioneers in the field of salt refining in Egypt. Our advanced facilities utilize imported processing machines from Germany and the USA, empowering us to wash, dry, and grade salt according to stringent international chemical parameters.

To satisfy escalating local and international demands, our high-tech infrastructure operates year-round. The strategic location of our primary refinery in Ismailia Governorate - Qantara El Gharbiyya gives us direct arterial access to the Suez Canal and Egypt's key maritime ports (Port Said, Damietta, Alexandria), guaranteeing swift vessel turnaround and reliable order fulfillment within stipulated timeframes.
