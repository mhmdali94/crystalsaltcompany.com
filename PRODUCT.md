# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Crystal Salt Industry Co. sells bulk salt to four audiences, all of them professional buyers rather than consumers:

- **Foreign importers and traders** in Europe, the Arabian Gulf, Africa and the Americas, comparing Egyptian suppliers for vessel loads and container orders.
- **Government bodies and tender committees**, mainly municipalities buying de-icing rock salt for winter roads.
- **Industrial buyers** (chemical, chlor-alkali, food processing, textile, water treatment) who need a specific grade that meets a written specification.
- **The local Egyptian and Arab market**: Arabic-speaking distributors, wholesalers and retail brands.

Their context: they are evaluating a supplier they have not worked with before, often on a phone, often on a slow connection, sometimes in their second language. They are looking for proof: purity figures, lab certificates, packaging options, loading ports, shipment history, and a real person to talk to.

## Product Purpose

The site is the company's credibility case. Its one job is to make a cautious buyer trust Crystal Salt enough to pick up the phone or open WhatsApp. Quote requests and brochure downloads follow from that trust; they are not the lead goal.

Success looks like a buyer who arrives skeptical, finds the facts they need for their grade and destination within a minute, and contacts the sales desk already convinced the company is a real, established manufacturer (not a middleman).

## Brand Personality

**Pure. Precise. Trusted.**

- **Pure**: the product is about purity (NaCl percentages, washing stages, whiteness). The site should feel clean and uncluttered in the same way.
- **Precise**: lead with exact figures, specifications and certificates rather than adjectives. Confidence comes from numbers and evidence.
- **Trusted**: an established Egyptian manufacturer since 2000, with a factory, ports, government partners and a named sales desk. Warm and personal enough that buyers feel they are dealing with people.

Voice: a knowledgeable, experienced exporter talking to a professional buyer. Factual, calm, courteous. Never salesy, never hype.

## Anti-references

- **Cheap Alibaba-style supplier pages**: cluttered layouts, stock photography, walls of badges, pop-ups, "best price!!" energy. This is the look of a trading middleman, the exact impression to avoid.
- **Over-designed or flashy sites**: heavy animation, effects and decoration that compete with the product facts and slow the page down.
- **Cold corporate sites**: faceless, stock-image, big-corporation pages with no story, no people and no sense of who answers the phone.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over claims.** Every promise should sit next to its proof: a purity figure, a lab certificate, a real photo of the factory or port, a shipment example. If something can't be shown, say less.
2. **Purity you can see.** The product is refined salt; the interface should feel refined too. Restraint, generous space and clear hierarchy signal quality better than decoration.
3. **Real people, real place.** Show the factory, the ports, the stockpiles and the sales desk. Make it obvious that a real Egyptian company with a phone number stands behind the site.
4. **Fast for everyone.** A buyer on a weak mobile connection in their second language must get to the facts quickly. Performance and clarity are part of the brand.
5. **Arabic and English as equals.** Both languages are first-class. Every layout, number and component must read naturally in RTL and LTR.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.2 AA**: text contrast, visible keyboard focus, full keyboard navigation, meaningful alt text on product and certificate images.
- **Slow mobile connections**: keep pages light; compress and lazy-load images and video; don't block content on heavy scripts or autoplay media.
- **Bilingual RTL/LTR**: Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR) are equal. Numbers, units, phone numbers and specifications must keep the correct reading order in both directions.
- **Non-native readers**: plain, direct wording; figures and units spelled out consistently.
