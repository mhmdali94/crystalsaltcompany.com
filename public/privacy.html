Commitment to Data Privacy

Welcome to the Privacy Policy page of Crystal Salt Industry Company (crystalsaltcompany.com). We place the utmost importance on your commercial privacy and strive to protect the contact information and technical inquiries you provide to us.

1. Data We Collect

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. When you use our quote request or contact forms, we collect basic business contact details (such as your name, corporate email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, and product specifications) solely to respond to your export inquiries. We also utilize Google Analytics to understand website traffic patterns, visitor geography, and device types to improve our web content.

2. Cookies & Technical Logs

Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to collect non-personally identifiable information regarding website usage trends. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings without impacting your ability to browse our salt products.

3. Embedded Content from External Providers

Articles and product pages on this site may feature embedded media (such as YouTube videos demonstrating salt production lines or Google Maps displaying our refinery location). These embedded services may collect non-personal device information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We never disclose or distribute your commercial data, price inquiries, or contact information to outside marketing third parties. Data is only shared with authorized logistics partners and maritime shipping agents necessary to fulfill executed sales contracts.

5. Contact Us Regarding Your Data

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to update or remove your business contact details from our records, please reach out to us:
