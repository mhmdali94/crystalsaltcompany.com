Tablet Salt Overview & Water Refining Applications

Tablet Salt is pressed from high-purity vacuum or double-washed salt under high mechanical compaction to form dense, uniform cylindrical briquettes. It is engineered specifically for regenerating ion-exchange resin beads in industrial and residential water softening systems without mushing or bridging in the brine tank.

Tablet salt is of critical importance in water refining processes, industrial boiler pre-treatment, hospital autoclaves, and commercial swimming pool chlorinators. It is also used in multiple countries as a prime source of industrial detergents and chemical synthesis.

Typical Chemical & Physical Specifications

Sodium Chloride (NaCl): ≥ 99.6% – 99.8%
Dissolution Behavior: 100% Residue-Free Dissolution
Calcium (Ca) & Magnesium (Mg): ≤ 0.05% Ultra-Low Hardness
Pellet Form: High-Density Compressed Pillow / Cylinder
