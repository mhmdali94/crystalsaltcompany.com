Double Washed Salt Overview & Advanced Purity

Double Washed Salt undergoes dual consecutive counter-current washing stages followed by centrifugal drying in our German-engineered processing lines. This rigorous treatment strips out virtually all magnesium, calcium, and insolubles, achieving a NaCl purity of 99.4% or higher.

Washed salt is used to recycle, increase processing efficiency, and produce premium table salt or serve demanding industrial applications such as chlor-alkali electrolysis, textile printing, and pharmaceutical excipients.

Typical Chemical & Physical Specifications

Sodium Chloride (NaCl): ≥ 99.4% – 99.6%
Moisture Content: ≤ 1.5% – 2.0%
Water Insolubles: ≤ 0.08%
Whiteness Index: 92%+ Bright Crystal
