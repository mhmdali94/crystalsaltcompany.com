How We Export

Bulk and container shipments from our refinery in Ismailia, shown with photos and documents from our own operations.

Bulk Vessel

Bulk Vessel Loading

Raw and rock salt loaded in bulk from the port stockpile into the vessel hold, for large orders such as winter road salt.

Loading ports: Port Said, Damietta, Alexandria
Terms: FOB or CIF
Best for: Raw and rock salt
Container

Container Shipments

Bagged salt loaded into containers for buyers who need smaller volumes or door-to-port delivery.

Loading: Bags on pallets in containers
Terms: FOB or CIF
Best for: Washed, food, kitchen and tablet salt
Packaging

Packaging to Your Order

Jumbo bags, woven bags and retail packs, packed and palletized at our facility in Ismailia.

Bulk: Jumbo bags
Industrial: Woven PP bags
Retail: Consumer packs, private label
