Rock Salt Overview & Winter Road De-Icing

Raw Rock Salt is utilized worldwide in the critical process of melting ice from roads, motorways, airport runways, and pedestrian pathways in countries where temperatures drop below zero and snowfall is abundant.

Salt is broadcast across highway surfaces before snowfall begins, forming an active saline brine layer that significantly lowers the freezing point of moisture. This delays or completely prevents hazardous ice bonding to the pavement, drastically reducing vehicular traffic accidents and highway slip collisions.
