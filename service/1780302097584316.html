Raw Salt Overview & Chemical Applications

Raw Salt produces all types of sea salt. Raw salt is harvested directly from pristine Egyptian salinas and is used for reuse in multiple countries to produce refined table salt through industrial recrystalization or mechanical washing.

It is also exported in substantial tonnage to European and North American municipalities for melting highway snow and ice. All specifications (NaCl purity, grain size curve, and moisture threshold) differ from one country to another and from one commercial application to another, and our refinery customizes every batch to match client contractual requirements.

Typical Chemical & Physical Specifications

Sodium Chloride (NaCl): ≥ 98.5% – 99.2%
Moisture Content: ≤ 2.5% – 3.5%
Water Insoluble Matter: ≤ 0.2%
Calcium (Ca) & Magnesium (Mg): Trace Mineral Standards
