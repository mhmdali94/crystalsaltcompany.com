Do You Have Any Questions?

Find quick answers regarding our products, chemical certifications, shipping logistics, and quality guarantees.

Do you provide your services throughout the week?
Yes, our commercial and shipping operations function 7 days a week, including holiday periods. Because maritime chartering operates on tight laytime windows, our team is always on duty to supervise port loading, documentation, and urgent vessel dispatches.
How much time does an export shipment order typically take?
It depends on the order volume and destination port. Containerized orders (FCL) are typically prepared and dispatched to Port Said or Damietta within 3 to 7 working days. Bulk cargo vessels (e.g., 5,000 to 50,000 tons of rock or raw salt) are loaded according to the agreed daily loading rate (WWDSHEX/SHINC) specified in the charter party agreement.
Do you offer free technical consultations before placing an order?
Yes, we provide free commercial and chemical consultations. Our salt engineers will review your required specifications (NaCl purity, grain size sieve curves, moisture thresholds, anti-caking additives) and provide certified batch test sheets to guarantee the correct grade for your industrial application.
Do you provide a formal guarantee for product quality?
Yes, we guarantee that all supplied salt strictly adheres to contractual specifications. Every batch is tested in accredited laboratories, and we welcome independent third-party survey inspections (such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) at the loading port before vessel departure.
Do you use safe materials and food-grade packaging equipment?
Yes, safety and hygiene are foundational to our operations. Our food-grade salt lines follow international Codex standards, using certified food-safe packaging with polyethylene moisture barriers. All equipment imported from Germany and the USA complies with sanitary handling protocols.
What shipping Incoterms and packaging options do you offer?
We provide flexible terms including FOB (Port Said, Damietta, Alexandria, Adabiya), CFR, and CIF to any global destination port. Packaging options include bulk in vessel holds, 1,000kg / 1,200kg UV-stabilized Jumbo bags, 25kg / 50kg polypropylene woven bags, or palletized retail packs.
