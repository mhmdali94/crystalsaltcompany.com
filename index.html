About Crystal Salt Co.

Salt Refining and Export Since 2000

Crystal Salt Industry Company is an established manufacturer, exporter, and global bulk supplier headquartered in Egypt. With over two decades of technical dedication, we specialize in producing and processing pure sea salt and rock salt for food, chemical, industrial, and water-treatment applications.

Our refinery is strategically located in Ismailia Governorate (Qantara El Gharb) near Egypt's primary sea channels, allowing rapid shipping through Suez Canal ports (Port Said, Damietta, and Alexandria) to our partners across the Mediterranean, Europe, the Arabian Gulf, and the Americas.

German & US Processing Lines: High-precision washing, centrifugal drying, and optical sorting machinery.
Maritime Export Capacity: Capability to fulfill bulk cargo vessel orders and containerized shipments on schedule.
Independently Tested: SGS Egypt measured our de-icing raw salt at 97.84% NaCl and 2.80% moisture (June 2023).
